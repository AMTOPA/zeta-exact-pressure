\begin{abstract}
We record a computer-assisted research-draft refinement of the positioned-pressure method for simple zeros of the Riemann zeta function on the critical line. The refinement retains exact shifted-block pressure accounting and a rigorous 15-term analytic window, but enlarges the local certificate from seven points to eight points and optimizes exact pair/position-pressure weights under the span-capacity constraints used by the predecessor deduction. A seven-dimensional outward-rounded interval computation proves the resulting local inequality. Subject to the same imported analytic interface and finite-dimensional Gram profile as the predecessor lineage, the certified numerical inputs yield
\[
\liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{N_0^s(T,2T)}{N(T,2T)}
\ge0.67337819547776013737\ldots,
\]
i.e. $67.3378195478\%$ before conservative decimal truncation. This manuscript is a research draft, not a peer-reviewed theorem and not a proof of the Riemann hypothesis.
\end{abstract}

\section{Imported analytic interface}

The exact pair weights are recorded in \texttt{candidate.json}. They are nonnegative and satisfy the span identities

\section{Exact shifted-pressure averaging}
For an $m$-point block there are $m-7$ translated eight-point windows. Summing their local inequalities yields the threshold
\[
A_m=\varepsilon_8(m-7).
\]
The span-capacity identities ensure that the summed pair contribution is dominated by the predecessor block pair energy, while exact shifted averaging gives pressure charge
\[
(m-7)B.
\]
Set
\[
R_m=h_m(A_m),\qquad \eta_m=\frac{R_m}{A_m}.
\]
The block-defect estimate becomes
\[
\Delta(M)\ge\frac{R_m}{m}S-\frac{\eta_mB(m-7)}{m}N-o(N),
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{\frac SN\ge\frac{mH_{\rm cert}-\eta_mB(m-7)}{m-R_m}-o(1).}
\]
