\begin{abstract}
We record a computer-assisted research-draft refinement of the positioned-pressure method for simple zeros of the Riemann zeta function on the critical line. In addition to optimizing a rational 15-term analytic window and exact local pair/position weights, the current construction releases the total local pressure itself as an optimization variable. The resulting seven-point, six-gap inequality is closed by an outward-rounded interval branch-and-bound computation. Subject to the imported analytic interface and finite-dimensional Gram profile of the predecessor lineage, the certified numerical inputs yield
\[
\liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{N_0^s(T,2T)}{N(T,2T)}
\ge0.67341308528684939167\ldots,
\]
i.e. $67.3413085287\%$ before conservative decimal truncation. This manuscript is a research draft, not a peer-reviewed theorem and not a proof of the Riemann hypothesis.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact shifted-pressure averaging}
For an $m$-point block there are $m-6$ translated seven-point windows. Set
\[
A_m=\varepsilon(m-6),\qquad
R_m=h_m(A_m),\qquad
\eta_m=\frac{R_m}{A_m}.
\]
The span-capacity identities control the summed pair contribution by the predecessor block energy, while exact shifted averaging gives pressure charge $(m-6)B$. Thus
\[
\Delta(M)\ge\frac{R_m}{m}S-\frac{\eta_mB(m-6)}{m}N-o(N),
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{\frac SN\ge\frac{mH_{\rm cert}-\eta_mB(m-6)}{m-R_m}-o(1).}
\]
