\begin{abstract}
We record a computer-assisted research-draft refinement of the positioned-pressure method for simple zeros of the Riemann zeta function on the critical line. The refinement retains exact shifted-block pressure accounting, uses a robust 15-term analytic window, and closes the resulting six-gap local inequality by outward-rounded interval branch-and-bound. Subject to the imported analytic interface and finite-dimensional Gram profile used by the predecessor lineage, the current certified numerical inputs yield
\[
\liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{N_0^s(T,2T)}{N(T,2T)}
\ge 0.67333146637444245098\ldots,
\]
i.e. $67.3331466374\%$ before conservative decimal truncation. This manuscript is a research draft, not a peer-reviewed theorem and not a proof of the Riemann hypothesis.
\end{abstract}

\section{Imported analytic interface}
Let $S=N_0^s(T,2T)$ and $N=N(T,2T)$. We import
\[
S\ge HN+\Delta(M)-o(N)
\]
and the finite-$m$ Gram profile
\[
h_m(E)=\begin{cases}
E,&0\le E\le m/(m-1),\\
E/m+2\sqrt{(m-1)E/m}-1,&E\ge m/(m-1).
\end{cases}
\]
These ingredients belong to the Anthropic / \texttt{trmdy} / \texttt{sxuff} lineage and are not reproved here.

\section{Exact shifted-pressure averaging}
For an $m$-point block, summing the seven-point inequality over the $m-6$ local windows gives total pressure charge
\[
(m-6)B.
\]
Across the shifted block partitions, exact double counting retains this factor rather than replacing it by a coarser $B(m-1)$ bound.

Set
\[
A_m=\varepsilon(m-6),\qquad R_m=h_m(A_m),\qquad \eta_m=\frac{R_m}{A_m}.
\]
Then the block-defect estimate gives
\[
\Delta(M)\ge\frac{R_m}{m}S-\frac{\eta_mB(m-6)}{m}N-o(N),
\]
and hence
\[
\boxed{\frac SN\ge\frac{mH_{\rm cert}-\eta_mB(m-6)}{m-R_m}-o(1).}
\]
